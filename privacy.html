Privacy Policy

Postcards of India Publisher · Effective 26 October 2026

What this tool is

Postcards of India Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Postcards of India YouTube channel to publish and manage that channel's videos. It uses YouTube API Services.

Use of YouTube API Services

By using this tool, the account owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

  • Channel information, video metadata and analytics for the Postcards of India channel only.
  • OAuth access tokens issued by Google, used to upload and update videos on that channel.

The tool does not access any other YouTube user's account, and does not collect personal information from viewers.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload, schedule and update the channel's videos and to report the channel's performance to its owner. Access tokens are stored in encrypted secret storage and are never shared, sold or used for advertising. Analytics summaries are kept only as long as needed for reporting.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties, except as needed to call Google services on the owner's behalf.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked.
