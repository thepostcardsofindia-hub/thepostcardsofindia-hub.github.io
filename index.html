Postcards of India

Short documentaries on India's places, history, heritage and surprising facts.

Postcards of India is a YouTube channel that explains why India looks, works and lives the way it does: its geography, forgotten places, monuments, cities and the stories behind them.

Watch the channel: youtube.com/@postcardsofindia

About the publishing tool

"Postcards of India Publisher" is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read analytics for the Postcards of India channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.
